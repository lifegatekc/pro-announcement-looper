# Privacy policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026. Applies to Pro Announcement Looper and Pro Announcement Screensaver. Publisher review is required before Store submission.

## Information used by the apps

The apps store the ProPresenter address you enter, presentation identifiers and names, display timing, schedules, refresh preferences, and optional remote-control preferences in Roku application storage. They download presentation information and slide images from the ProPresenter server you configure. Images are cached on the Roku and may be removed by the Roku operating system.

These settings and images are not sent to a developer-operated service. The apps do not contain advertising, purchase processing, analytics, a registration system, or a developer-operated cloud backend. The bundled offline demo uses local sample images.

When enabled, the regular player accepts local-network commands through Roku's External Control Protocol. These commands can choose or refresh a presentation or resume a schedule. The apps do not add authentication to Roku's local control interface. Use a trusted network and Roku's network-control settings.

## Local exit diagnostics

At startup, the apps may retain up to eight recent exit records per app and entry point on the Roku: exit code, timestamp, foreground/background state, media-player state, memory limit when available, and the version collecting the report. Diagnostics exclude raw console text, presentation content, names, and server addresses. They are not shown on the TV or automatically uploaded. A developer with authorized device access can inspect or export them for troubleshooting. These records are held in a registry section separate from presentation settings.

## Your choices

Remote actions are off by default. Automatic refresh intervals can be set to On open only. You can remove a presentation from the app, change its source, or uninstall the app. Removing a presentation removes its configuration and switch times from the list; cached files may remain until normal cache cleanup or operating-system eviction. Uninstalling does not delete any source material from ProPresenter. Storage shared by apps signed with the same developer key is governed by Roku's platform behavior.

Roku and your ProPresenter provider may process information under their own policies. If you open the project's GitHub pages or submit an issue, GitHub processes that interaction under its policy. Do not include private slide content, passwords, or network details in public issues.
